# Hearthleaf

Hearthleaf is a fictional online tea shop run by four people. Alex is the founder. Bri buys the tea. Cam runs shop operations. Nora designs packaging and is not on staff.

The shop sells small lots of loose tea and seasonal tins. The sample domain is hearthleaf.example.test. No real company uses this name in this sample.
