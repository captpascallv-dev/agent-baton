# How we work

Call `company()` before answering questions about the company. Do not invent status, role holders, or reasons.

Append progress with `update_project_status`. Change the sections 现状, 下一步, 分工, or 入口 with `update_project_summary`. Record a decision with `record_decision`, and include the reason. Replacing the holder of a key role waits for the owner's approval.

Write where a credential lives and what it is called. Do not write the value.

The owner approves anything that is sent outside, published, paid for, or deleted, and any change to credentials or real data.

Add each agent as its own member under `people/`. Set `ONESTEAD_AGENT` to that member's key when you connect.
