## 社区角色 / Community characters

研究室里的六位 AI 娘都是社区创作的非官方 AI 拟人形象。我们以原设计为参考，用 AI 重新绘制了 Q 版与立绘，并放进同一间研究室场景；姿态、服装细节、配色和场景均有改动。改编后的素材以 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 发布，仅限非商业使用。

The six AI girls in the studio are unofficial, community-made personifications of AI models. Using the original designs as references, we re-drew chibi sprites and portraits with AI image generation and placed them in one shared studio scene, with changes to poses, costume details, colors and setting. The adapted assets are released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, for non-commercial use only.
